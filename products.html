What we trade

Dates, Coffee & Olive Oil

SIBT trades three natural crop products, each moved from origin to market with the same trade discipline — supplied locally into Oman and exported to international buyers, with documentation and packaging handled at every step.

01 / DATES

Dates

Omani Fardh and Khalas dates sourced for both bulk local trade and retail-ready export, alongside Deglet Noor imported from North Africa for buyers seeking that classic translucent, amber presentation. Supplied loose in bulk cartons for food-service and repacking, or in retail formats for supermarket and gift channels.

View Dates
02 / COFFEE

Coffee

A fully documented lot list of Yemen green Arabica across four highland districts — Hayma, Haraz, Bani Matar and Khawlan — offered natural and anaerobic processed, graded Gold through Classic, and shipped in GrainPro-lined 50kg export bags with certificates of origin available on request.

View Coffee
03 / OLIVE OIL

Olive Oil

Extra virgin olive oil imported from established North African groves, selected on acidity, aroma and colour for genuine culinary quality. Offered in bulk for local food-service and repacking, and in export-ready formats for retail shelf placement.

View Olive Oil

Supply model

One sourcing desk, two supply routes

Every product SIBT trades runs through the same dual supply model — moving into Oman's own retail and food-service market, and out to international buyers through documented export.

Local Supply — Oman

Dates, coffee and olive oil supplied directly into Oman's retail, wholesale and food-service channels, with bulk formats available for repacking and private label under an Omani buyer's own brand.

Export — International Markets

The same lots are prepared for overseas buyers, with export documentation, Certificate of Origin and quality certificates arranged on request to support customs clearance in the destination market.

How we work

Sourcing, documentation and delivery, handled together

01

Sourcing

Direct relationships with Omani growers and established North African and Yemeni origins across dates, olive oil and coffee.

02

03
