Omani & Imported Dates

SIBT supplies Fardh and Khalas dates grown in Oman alongside Deglet Noor imported from North Africa — moved as bulk lots for local trade and packed for retail and export.

View Varieties Request Trade Info

Varieties

Three dates, three characters

Omani variety

Fardh

One of Oman's most widely grown dates — semi-dry with an amber-brown skin and firm, sweet flesh. Its low moisture content gives Fardh excellent keeping quality, making it a dependable choice for everyday local consumption and longer supply chains alike.

Omani variety

Khalas

A soft-textured, golden-amber date and one of the most popular varieties across the Gulf. Khalas is prized for its rich, caramel-like sweetness and tender bite, and moves readily in both the local Omani market and regional trade.

Imported — North Africa

Deglet Noor

The signature North African export variety — semi-dry with a translucent, light-amber flesh that holds its shape and shine. Deglet Noor is prized for its appearance and shelf presentation, making it a strong fit for retail and gifting formats.

Supply model

Supplied locally, exported internationally

Local Supply — Oman

SIBT moves Fardh, Khalas and Deglet Noor dates into Oman's retail, wholesale and food-service channels, with bulk cartons available for repacking and private label under an Omani buyer's own brand.

Export — International Markets

The same varieties are prepared for international buyers, with export documentation, Certificate of Origin and quality certificates arranged on request to support customs clearance in the destination market.

Packaging

From bulk carton to retail shelf

SIBT supplies dates in bulk cartons suited to food-service kitchens and repackers, as well as retail-ready formats — sealed pouches, gift boxes and private-label packaging — for buyers who want to bring a finished product to shelf.

  • Bulk cartons for food-service and repacking
  • Retail-ready pouches and gift boxes
  • Private-label potential, discussed per buyer
