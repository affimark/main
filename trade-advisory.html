Trade Advisory

Business Mediation & Trade Advisory

Alongside our trade in Dates, Coffee and Olive Oil, SIBT runs a mediation desk that connects companies and explores markets between Oman and the world — helping businesses on both sides navigate entry and find the right partner.

Our Services Start a Conversation

Overview

Connecting companies, exploring markets

Whether you're a company outside Oman looking to enter the Omani market, or an Omani or regional business exploring opportunities abroad, SIBT's trade advisory desk helps you navigate the process. We work as a mediation point between both sides — understanding what each party needs, identifying who they should be talking to, and helping the arrangement take practical shape.

This sits alongside, and draws on, our own experience moving Dates, Coffee and Olive Oil between origin and market — so our advice is grounded in how trade actually moves, not just how it's planned.

What we offer

Three areas of support

01

Market entry support

Helping companies from outside Oman enter the Omani market, and helping Omani and regional companies explore international markets — understanding the landscape before committing to it.

02

Partner alignment & introductions

Identifying the right counterparts on each side of a potential deal, and making the introductions — so conversations start with the people who are actually the right fit.

03

Deal & logistics support

Helping structure how an arrangement comes together, and coordinating the practical and logistics side of getting a deal from agreement to execution.

How it works

A typical engagement

01

Understand the objective

We start by understanding what you're trying to achieve — entering a market, finding a partner, or moving a product — and what success looks like.

02

Identify counterparts & markets

We look at who or where fits that objective, drawing on our own trade network and market knowledge between Oman and abroad.

03

Facilitate introductions & alignment

We make the introductions and help both sides align on what a workable arrangement would look like, in plain and practical terms.

04

Support through to execution

We stay involved as the arrangement takes shape, helping coordinate the practical and logistics detail so it holds together in practice.
