About SIBT

A Muscat trading house, built on three origins

Sohar International Business & Trade LLC moves dates, coffee and olive oil between origin and market, and runs a trade mediation desk that connects companies across borders — all from a single home port on Oman's trade coast.

Our position

Between origin and the world, by geography and by design

Oman sits at a natural crossing point for the products SIBT trades. Yemen's highland coffee districts lie a short sea and land route from Muscat. North Africa's date gardens and olive groves connect through established regional shipping lanes. And Sohar and Muscat's ports sit directly on the east-west shipping corridors that carry cargo on to Asia, Europe and beyond.

That position is only useful when paired with a stable, trade-friendly base — and Oman's political stability, straightforward port infrastructure and openness to international trade give SIBT a dependable platform to source consistently and ship reliably, year after year.

What ties it together

Authentic origin, held to one standard of trade discipline

Traceable to source

Every lot SIBT moves — Omani date gardens, Yemen's highland coffee districts, North African olive groves — is traceable back to where it was grown, not simply bought on the open market.

Documentation first

Certificates of origin, quality documentation and clean paperwork travel with every shipment, so buyers can move goods through customs and retail channels without friction.

Packaging & logistics rigor

From bulk sacks to retail-ready packs, SIBT handles packaging and freight to the standard international buyers and Omani retailers expect on arrival.

This same discipline — traceability, documentation, careful handling — is what carries across into our trade mediation practice. When we introduce a partner or help a company enter a new market, we apply the same standard of rigor we use for our own cargo.
