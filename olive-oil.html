Extra Virgin Olive Oil, North African Origin

SIBT imports extra virgin olive oil from North Africa, selected for exceptional quality — low acidity, fresh cold-extraction character and careful handling from grove to shipment — supplied locally in Oman and exported internationally.

Our Quality Standard Request Trade Terms

Origin & positioning

Selected for quality, not volume

Our extra virgin olive oil is sourced from established North African olive-growing regions and positioned squarely on quality rather than price competition. Every shipment is chosen for its low acidity, clean fruit character and fresh-harvest condition — the qualities that distinguish a genuine extra virgin grade from a merely acceptable one.

SIBT works directly with origin suppliers, allowing us to trace each lot back to its harvest season and confirm extraction method before it is offered to buyers in Oman or abroad.

What extra virgin means

Quality built in at origin, not added later

01

Harvest timing

Olives are picked at optimal ripeness within a tight harvest window, rather than left to over-ripen — the single biggest factor in final oil quality.

02

Cold extraction

Oil is mechanically extracted without heat or chemical solvents, preserving the natural aroma, flavour and polyphenol content of the fruit.

03

Low acidity

Free acidity is kept well within extra virgin limits — a reliable marker of clean, careful extraction from sound, fresh-pressed fruit.

04

Storage & handling

Oil is held in temperature-controlled, light-protected conditions from origin to shipment, guarding freshness until it reaches the buyer.

Local & export supply

Supplied within Oman, and exported abroad

Local supply — Oman

We supply extra virgin olive oil to retailers, foodservice buyers and distributors across Oman, in both bottled and bulk formats to suit local demand.

Export

Beyond Oman, we export to international buyers looking for a dependable, quality-graded North African source — packed in bottle or bulk tin for import.

Formats

Bottled for retail, bulk for trade buyers

Olive oil is offered in retail-ready bottles for local shelf placement and foodservice use, and in bulk tins for import buyers and repackers who handle their own final packaging. Format and order size are confirmed directly with our trade desk — no pricing is published, as terms depend on volume, format and destination.
