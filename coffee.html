02 / Products — Coffee

Yemen Green Coffee

A wide range of Arabica heirloom varieties from Yemen's highland coffee districts — natural and anaerobic processed, and offered to roasters and importers as a fully documented lot list.

View Lot List Request Offer Sheet

Origin districts

Four highland regions, one heirloom heritage

Hayma

One of Yemen's most storied coffee districts — terraced gardens above 1,700 masl producing the Haimi lots: Gold, Silver, Odini and Classic.

Haraz

Historic coffee mountains west of Sana'a, home to the Harazi lots and the single-village Nahmi natural and anaerobic selections.

Bani Matar

High terraces feeding the Matari lots, prized for structured, heirloom Arabica character across three grading tiers.

Khawlan

Terraces producing the natural-processed Khawlani Classic, alongside contributions to SIBT's multi-region Export Blend.

Elevation profile

Grown high, across 1,700–2,500 masl

Processing

Natural & anaerobic processing

01

Natural

Cherries are dried whole in the sun on raised beds, developing the fruit-forward, heavy-body character Yemen coffee is known for.

02

Anaerobic

Cherries ferment in sealed, oxygen-free tanks before drying — a controlled process producing cleaner, more complex cup profiles.

03

Grading tiers

Within each region, lots are graded Gold, Silver and Classic by cup score — Gold the highest tier, Classic the standard quality grade. Single-village selections such as Odini and the Nahmi lots are offered on their own merits.
